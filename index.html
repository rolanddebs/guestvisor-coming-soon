How GuestVisor is designed to work.

After a participating hotel verifies a stay, GuestVisor is designed to bring the traveller's pre-arrival information to the people preparing for their welcome. The hotel remains in control of all decisions.

The hotel invites the traveller.

The hotel verifies the reservation and sends a secure invitation for the traveller to prepare for their stay.

The traveller shares what matters.

Arrival details, relevant preferences and specific requests can be shared before check-in.

The hotel team prepares and responds.

Front Office receives a clear briefing, coordinates with the appropriate departments and confirms only what the hotel can arrange.
